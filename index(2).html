SACRED CHRONICLE

HISTORY OF SRI SATHYA SAI SHARADANIKETANAM

From divine conception in 2000 to the grand centennial era of 2026: Explore the chronological milestones of love, service, and values-based education.

YEAR 2000 • SACRED GENESIS

DIVINE CONCEPTION & INSPIRATION

The sacred vision for a values-based Gurukulam in rural Mandya was envisioned to offer completely free residential education that blends spiritual sanctity with modern academic excellence.

"The idea was inspired by Sadguru Sri Madhusudan Sai."

YEAR 2013

DIVINE FOUNDATION STONE & INAUGURATION

Sadguru Sri Madhusudan Sai laid the sacred foundation stone (Shilanyasa) of Sri Sathya Sai Sharadaniketanam in Mandya, dedicating the campus to the rural children of Karnataka with zero tuition or residential fees.

YEAR 2014

FIRST INTAKE OF GURUKULAM STUDENTS

Welcoming the inaugural batch of rural boys into Grade 6. The Gurukula routine of morning prayers, yoga, nourishing vegetarian food, and values education was formally established.

YEAR 2015

ACADEMIC BLOCK DEDICATION & VEDA TRADITION

Inauguration of spacious classrooms, prayer assembly hall, and regular Vedic chanting training. Students began excelling in district Sanskrit and cultural competitions.

YEAR 2016

CONSECRATION OF DHYANA MANTAPA & TEMPLES

Consecration of the Dhyana Mantapa hilltop meditation sanctuary along with the sanctums of Sri Gavisiddeshwara and Sri Maramma Devi, blessing the campus with divine spiritual peace.

YEAR 2017

NIOS CURRICULUM ACCREDITATION

Official accreditation under National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) curriculum, enabling holistic, flexible, and high-standard academic certification for rural youth.

YEAR 2018

100% DISTINCTION IN SECONDARY EXAMS

The first batch of Grade 10 students achieved a 100% first-class pass rate, setting a lasting benchmark of academic excellence coupled with character building.

YEAR 2019

SMART CLASSROOMS & SCIENCE LABORATORIES

Modern science laboratories and digital multimedia rooms installed, bridging the rural-urban educational divide with modern computing equipment.

YEAR 2020

RESILIENT DIGITAL LEARNING & COVID RELIEF SEVA

Unbroken continuity of learning through virtual setups, alongside large-scale Grama Seva grocery and healthcare relief distribution to surrounding Mandya villages.

YEAR 2021

SAI CHAYA HOSTEL & SARASWATI CENTRAL LIBRARY

Inauguration of the modern residential Sai Chaya Hostel block and the Saraswati Central Library, offering a serene sanctuary for research and contemplative reading.

YEAR 2022

SENIOR SECONDARY (GRADE 11 & 12) LAUNCH

Expansion into Senior Secondary education under Humanities stream, preparing young men for civil services, higher academia, and social leadership.

YEAR 2023

SAI SWASTHYA WELLNESS HEALTHCARE INAUGURATION

Opening of Sai Swasthya rural healthcare center providing 100% free medical care, dental screenings, and routine health checks for campus students and local villagers.

YEAR 2024

WORLD ENVIRONMENT DAY & GREEN GURUKULAM DRIVE

Major sapling plantation and environmental conservation drive on June 5, 2024, expanding green cover, solar utilities, and organic vegetable cultivation across the campus.

YEAR 2025

SANCHARI MITHRA REBOOT LAUNCH

Introduction of SANCHARI MITHRA REBOOT — the official campus robot companion and AI guide, integrating campus heritage, day-to-day schedules, and visitor guidance.

YEAR 2026 • CENTENARY ERA

100TH BIRTHDAY OF BHAGAVAN SRI SATHYA SAI BABA

Grand centenary celebrations of Bhagavan Sri Sathya Sai Baba and next-phase Gurukulam campus expansion, furthering the mission of universal love, truth, and free education.
