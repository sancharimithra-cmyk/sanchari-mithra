SACRED CHRONICLE

HISTORY OF SRI SATHYA SAI SHARADANIKETANAM

● ● ●

From divine conception in 2000 to the grand centennial era of 2026: Explore the chronological milestones of love, service, and values-based education.

2000
YEAR 2000 DIVINE GENESIS

DIVINE CONCEPTION & INSPIRATION

What Happened

The sacred vision for a values-based Gurukulam in rural Mandya was envisioned to offer completely free residential education blending spiritual sanctity with modern academic excellence.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Established the foundational principle of character-based education, ensuring every child receives top-tier schooling with zero financial burden.

"The idea was inspired by Sadhguru Sri Madhusudan Sai."

2013
YEAR 2013 FOUNDATION ERA

DIVINE FOUNDATION STONE & INAUGURATION

What Happened

Sadhguru Sri Madhusudan Sai laid the sacred foundation stone (Shilanyasa) of Sri Sathya Sai Sharadaniketanam in Mandya.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Formally dedicated the campus to the rural children of Karnataka with zero tuition or residential fees, marking the dawn of modern Gurukula learning in the region.

2014
YEAR 2014 FIRST INTAKE

FIRST INTAKE OF GURUKULAM STUDENTS

What Happened

Welcoming the inaugural batch of rural boys into Grade 6 under the caring embrace of dedicated resident teachers.

Why It Mattered & Impact

The Gurukula discipline of early dawn prayers, yoga, nourishing vegetarian diet, and moral values was formally instituted into daily life.

2015
YEAR 2015 ACADEMIC WING

ACADEMIC BLOCK DEDICATION & VEDA TRADITION

What Happened

Inauguration of spacious classrooms, prayer assembly hall, and comprehensive Vedic chanting education.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Students began excelling across district Sanskrit, academic, and cultural competitions, gaining widespread admiration for poise and character.

2016
YEAR 2016 SACRED SANCTUARIES

CONSECRATION OF DHYANA MANTAPA & TEMPLES

What Happened

Consecration of the Dhyana Mantapa hilltop meditation sanctuary along with the sanctums of Sri Gavisiddeshwara and Sri Maramma Devi.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Bestowed a powerful spiritual heartbeat on campus, creating an oasis of meditation and quiet inner reflection for boys growing up in nature.

2017
YEAR 2017 ACCREDITATION

NIOS CURRICULUM ACCREDITATION

What Happened

Official curriculum accreditation under the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS), Ministry of Education, Government of India.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Provided high-standard, flexible national academic certification tailored to unlock the boundless inner potential of rural youngsters.

2018
YEAR 2018 ACADEMIC TRIUMPH

100% DISTINCTION IN SECONDARY EXAMS

What Happened

The pioneer batch of Grade 10 students appeared for secondary board examinations with 100% first-class distinction pass rate.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Validated the Gurukulam model: academic excellence and moral rectitude seamlessly reinforce one another to produce remarkable young citizens.

2019
YEAR 2019 DIGITAL CAMPUS

SMART CLASSROOMS & SCIENCE LABORATORIES

What Happened

Installation of modern science laboratories and digital multimedia interactive suites across campus.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Bridged the digital divide, equipping rural students with state-of-the-art computers, coding fundamentals, and scientific inquiry tools.

2020
YEAR 2020 RESILIENT SEVA

RESILIENT DIGITAL LEARNING & COVID RELIEF SEVA

What Happened

Unbroken continuity of learning through digital setups alongside large-scale Grama Seva grocery and healthcare relief distribution.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Staff and alumni distributed relief packages across neighboring Mandya villages during testing times, exemplifying selfless love in action.

2021
YEAR 2021 CAMPUS EXPANSION

SAI CHAYA HOSTEL & SARASWATI CENTRAL LIBRARY

What Happened

Inauguration of the modern residential Sai Chaya Hostel block and the expansive Saraswati Central Library.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Offered world-class residential boarding and a treasure-house of literature, science, and sacred scriptures for research and reading.

2022
YEAR 2022 SENIOR SECONDARY

SENIOR SECONDARY (GRADE 11 & 12) LAUNCH

What Happened

Expansion into Senior Secondary education under the Humanities stream for Grade 11 and 12 scholars.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Prepares mature young men for civil services, higher academia, and compassionate leadership in national public service.

2023
YEAR 2023 HEALTHCARE SEVA

SAI SWASTHYA WELLNESS HEALTHCARE INAUGURATION

What Happened

Opening of the Sai Swasthya rural healthcare center providing 100% free medical care, dental screenings, and routine wellness checkups.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Ensures vibrant health for all campus students while extending compassionate healing to hundreds of rural families in surrounding Mandya taluks.

2024
YEAR 2024 ENVIRONMENTAL SEVA

WORLD ENVIRONMENT DAY & GREEN GURUKULAM DRIVE

What Happened

Major sapling plantation and environmental conservation drive on June 5, 2024, expanding green canopy and solar utilities.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Fosters deep ecological awareness among students through hands-on organic farming, water harvesting, and renewable campus energy.

2025
YEAR 2025 INNOVATION & AI

SANCHARI MITHRA REBOOT LAUNCH

What Happened

Introduction of SANCHARI MITHRA REBOOT - the official campus robot companion and AI guide.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Integrates campus heritage, daily routines, photo archives, and voice guidance into an advanced digital assistant for visitors, parents, and students.

2026
YEAR 2026 CENTENARY ERA

100TH BIRTHDAY OF BHAGAVAN SRI SATHYA SAI BABA

What Happened

Grand centenary celebrations of Bhagavan Sri Sathya Sai Baba and next-phase Gurukulam campus expansion.

Why It Mattered & Impact

Furthering the divine mission of universal love, truth, and free values-based education, illuminating thousands of young lives across generations.
